# Original CAD examples

`mounting-kit.step` is an original example generated by TechDraw with Replicad / OpenCascade. It contains an 80 × 50 × 12 mm mounting plate with a 16 mm central through bore, four 6 mm mounting holes, and a separate 24 mm outside diameter / 10 mm inside diameter / 20 mm high spacer. All dimensions are nominal model geometry. The example is released under CC0-1.0.

The example is intentionally a two-solid assembly so that component selection and batch PDF export can be exercised with real STEP data.

`nested-assembly.step` is a separate original CC0-1.0 example generated with the OpenCascade XCAF assembly writer. It contains a 10 mm cube nested under two named assemblies and placed at X = 100 mm, Y = 20 mm. It exercises semantic product paths and world-space component placement.

`inspection-plate.dxf` is an original ASCII DXF authored for TechDraw and released under CC0-1.0. It declares millimetres (`$INSUNITS = 4`) and contains one 70 × 40 mm planar outline with four R5 corners encoded as LWPOLYLINE bulges, two Ø6 holes at (15, 20) and (55, 20), and a Ø16 bore at (35, 20). A source TEXT note describes the bores. It is a 2D drawing and supplies no thickness or invented 3D views. Its entities follow Autodesk's published DXF group-code reference for [LWPOLYLINE](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2024/ENU/AutoCAD-DXF/files/GUID-748FC305-F3F2-4F74-825A-61F04D757A50.htm) and [bulge values](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2024/ENU/AutoCAD-DXF/files/GUID-0741E831-599E-4CBF-91E1-8ADBCFD6556D.htm).
